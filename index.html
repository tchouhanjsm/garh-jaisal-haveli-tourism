Is Garh Jaisal Haveli inside Jaisalmer Fort?+

Yes. We are located inside the historic fort in Kotari Para, Jaisalmer. The lanes are narrow and vehicles cannot reach the hotel door, so please read our arrival guide before travelling.

Can you help with luggage on arrival?+

Luggage assistance is not guaranteed. Use the verified contact route from your booking confirmation to ask whether help can be arranged for your arrival.

The Experiences page compares six source-listed options. Current operating status, routes, guides, transport and inclusions are not live on this site; use a verified contact route from your booking confirmation to confirm what can be arranged for your dates.
