Questions worth asking

Before you set off.

How is the Swiss tent different from sleeping under the stars?+

The source describes a tent room with a king bed and attached bathroom for the Swiss-tent option; Sleep Under the Stars is an open-air setup. Confirm current facilities for your booking.

Are all meals included?+

High tea, dinner and breakfast are listed in the source record. Confirm the final inclusions, menus, beverages and any extras.

Is a separate jeep safari included?+

The source exclusion list says a separate jeep safari excursion must be booked separately. Confirm this distinction and the total price in writing.
