Practical considerations

Good to know before booking.

  • Ask about bathroom access and privacy before booking; do not assume an attached bathroom.
  • Ask about bedding, temperatures, weather cancellation and alternative arrangements for winter or rain.
  • Confirm walking over sand, mobility constraints, pickup, included meals, fees and cancellation terms.

Questions worth asking

Before you set off.

Where do guests sleep?+

The source describes an open-air setup with a mattress and blankets under the sky, rather than a tent room.

Is there an attached bathroom?+

The source says this is not the same setup as a Swiss Deluxe Tent. Ask the hotel to explain the current bathroom arrangement before booking.

What about cold desert nights?+

The source advises that winter nights can be cold. Confirm bedding, weather contingencies and the current overnight setup.
