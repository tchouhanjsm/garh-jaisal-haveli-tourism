Vehicle arrangements · custom route discussion

Go beyond the fort lanes.

The legacy site describes SUV/sedan arrangements across Rajasthan and Delhi, and suggests several themed routes to discuss. Vehicle availability, pickup, destinations, travel time and prices must be confirmed for your dates.

What the source describes

Start with the route
you have in mind.

The legacy car-tour page separates the existing guided Jaisalmer options from longer vehicle arrangements and proposed themes. The themes below are enquiry ideas, not confirmed or bookable tour packages.

01

SUV or sedan arrangements

The source lists vehicle arrangements for pickup/drop-off and intercity journeys across Rajasthan and Delhi. Confirm vehicle type, provider, pickup point, passenger/luggage capacity, fare, timings and availability.

02

Historic architecture

Ask whether a route around carved sandstone havelis or heritage buildings can be arranged. Stops and access depend on the agreed itinerary and current rules.

03

Jain temples inside the Fort

The source research proposes an architecture-led Fort walk around the Jain temples and Palace Museum. Visitor access, opening hours, temple rules and guide availability must be checked.

04

Lake and cenotaph viewpoints

Gadisar Lake, Bada Bagh and Vyas Chhatri appear in the source as possible photography/heritage themes. They may need to be planned as separate outings; do not assume they fit one standard tour.

05

Kuldhara and the desert edge

The source suggests a heritage outing around Kuldhara and the surrounding landscape. Confirm daylight access, visitor rules, route, transport, total time and current fees.

06

Fossils or desert ecology

The source suggests asking about Akal Wood Fossil Park or a suitable Desert National Park-area visit. Exact access, area, route and any wildlife-viewing expectations need confirmation; sightings are never guaranteed.

07

Longewala War Memorial

Longewala appears as a research/draft idea in the legacy repository, not as a confirmed package. Do not rely on availability, timing, permitted visits or price until the hotel confirms the full route and feasibility.

Custom route enquiry

Share your starting point
and your priorities.

Tell the hotel your date, pickup point, destinations, passenger count and whether you need a one-way transfer, return journey or guided visit. Ask for a written route, total fare, timings, inclusions and cancellation terms before agreeing.
