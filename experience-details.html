Afternoon to evening · source-listed outline

Sunset camel safari

The source describes a same-evening desert outing by jeep, a camel ride, sunset refreshments and dinner before returning to Jaisalmer. This is an outline, not a confirmed timetable or guarantee of current operation.

Typical sequence

  1. Leave Jaisalmer by jeep. Travel towards the desert route.
  2. Khuldhara and local stops. The source describes Khuldhara, a natural pond and a nomadic-village route.
  3. Ride by camel. Continue towards the dunes under the confirmed package plan.
  4. Watch the sunset. The source lists tea or coffee and snacks as the light changes over the dunes.
  5. Dinner and return. The source describes dinner prepared in the desert, followed by a return to Jaisalmer the same evening.

What the source lists

  • Jeep transport and route stops
  • Camel riding
  • Tea, coffee and snacks at sunset
  • Dinner prepared in the desert

Confirm before agreeing

Ask about route and stop access, pickup point, departure and return window, camel-ride duration, total price, exclusions and cancellation terms. Sunset timing varies by season.

Overnight · open sky

Sleep under the stars

The current source lists one night in an open-air sleeping arrangement with a mattress and blankets, plus a camel ride, dinner, breakfast and jeep transport. It is not a Swiss Deluxe Tent. Confirm current operation, bathroom access, bedding and cold-weather arrangements for your date.

Typical sequence

  1. Depart by jeep. Travel from Jaisalmer towards the desert.
  2. Local route stops. The source describes Khuldhara, a natural pond and a nomadic-village route.
  3. Camel ride and sunset. Ride towards the dunes, then have tea, coffee and snacks as the sun sets.
  4. Desert dinner. The source lists a pure-vegetarian dinner prepared in the desert.
  5. Sleep outdoors. The listed setup is a mattress and blankets beneath the open sky.
  6. Breakfast and return. The source describes breakfast the following morning before returning to Jaisalmer.

What the source lists

  • Jeep transport and route stops
  • Camel riding and sunset refreshments
  • Pure-vegetarian dinner and morning breakfast
  • Mattress and blankets for the open-air sleeping setup

Important to confirm

Ask what bathroom access is available, what bedding is supplied, and what happens in cold or unsuitable weather. The source distinguishes this from the attached bathroom setup listed for a Swiss Deluxe Tent.

Guided heritage · half-day

Half-day guided Jaisalmer tour

The source describes a shorter guided introduction for visitors with limited time. The exact route is adapted to the agreed plan, opening hours, weather and guide availability.

Typical sequence

  1. Meet at the haveli. The source lists Garh Jaisal Haveli inside Jaisalmer Fort as the planned starting point, subject to confirmation.
  2. Walk through the Fort. Explore lanes, architecture and the living-fort setting.
  3. Visit selected havelis. Agree which heritage and architectural stops suit the route.
  4. Explore markets and old-city lanes. Allow time for questions and photographs along the agreed route.
  5. Finish at the agreed point. Confirm the end point before setting out.

Source-listed inclusions

  • Local guide
  • Guided walking route through heritage areas
  • Fort and heritage context
  • Stops at selected havelis and markets according to the agreed itinerary

Fees and language

Road travel · enquiry only

Car and road travel arrangements

The owner-provided source lists SUV and sedan arrangements for journeys across Rajasthan and Delhi, including desert pickup or drop-off and intercity routes. This is not a quote or confirmation that a particular vehicle is available.

What to include in your request

  1. Travel date. Share the date you need the journey, without assuming a vehicle is reserved.
  2. Pickup and destination. Give exact locations and planned stops so the route can be checked.
  3. Passengers and luggage. State traveller count and luggage needs so the vehicle can be discussed.
  4. Vehicle preference. Tell the hotel whether you would prefer an SUV or sedan.
  5. Confirm the quote. Ask for route, pickup window, vehicle, inclusions and total fare in writing before agreeing.

Source-listed details

  • SUV and sedan arrangements by enquiry
  • Pickup or drop-off from desert locations
  • Intercity journeys across Rajasthan and Delhi
  • Drivers who can communicate in Hindi and English

Availability and fare

Vehicle availability, route, pickup timing, luggage capacity and price must be confirmed for the requested journey.
