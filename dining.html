A rooftop meal is more than a pause between sightseeing. It is a chance to watch the sandstone city change colour around you.

At Garh Jaisal’s Kitchen, the setting is part of the occasion: open skies, traditional architecture and the rooftops of historic Jaisalmer stretching into the distance. Join us for a relaxed meal, a quiet conversation or an evening you have planned just for two.

Menu choices and availability can vary. Contact us before visiting for current food options, service times and any dietary requests.
