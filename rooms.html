Before you choose

Compare the details, not just the photograph.

Room photos help you understand the haveli’s character, but they do not confirm which room is available or which exact layout will be assigned. Ask for the room name and written details for your dates before you commit.

Questions to settle

Confirm the bed arrangement, maximum occupancy, bathroom arrangement, cooling, view, stairs or access considerations, included items, taxes, cancellation terms and total rate. These details are not presented here as a live inventory.

Tell us what matters

Share who is travelling, your preferred bed setup, any mobility needs and the dates you have in mind. Ask whether the pictured room is the specific option being offered and what is included in that rate.
