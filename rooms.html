Garh Jaisal Haveli is inside Jaisalmer Fort, where vehicles cannot reach the hotel door. Plan for a final walk through the fort lanes and consider luggage or mobility requirements before booking. Plan with confidence

Choose with context,
not assumptions.

Room fit

Tell the team who is travelling and whether you need a king bed, second bed, window seat or a specific floor. Ask for confirmed occupancy and current amenities before deciding.

Arrival access

From Fort Parking, allow about 300 metres (around 5–6 minutes on foot); from the fort entrance, about 200 metres (around 3–4 minutes). These are estimates, and busy lanes can take longer. Confirm where to stop, how to reach the haveli and whether any assistance is available for your date.
