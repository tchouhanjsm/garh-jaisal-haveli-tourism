Settle the important
details in advance.

Ask the team to confirm the exact room name, bed arrangement and maximum occupancy; which listed amenities are currently provided; the total rate and applicable taxes; cancellation terms; and what is included in the quoted price. This page does not represent a live inventory system.

Garh Jaisal Haveli is inside Jaisalmer Fort, where vehicles cannot reach the hotel door. Plan for a final walk through the fort lanes and consider luggage or mobility requirements before booking.
