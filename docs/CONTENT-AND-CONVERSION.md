# Content and conversion plan

## Positioning

**Core promise:** Stay inside the Golden Fort and experience Jaisalmer with heritage character, rooftop views and warm local guidance.

**Primary audience:** Domestic and international travellers building a Rajasthan itinerary, couples planning a memorable stay, heritage and culture travellers, photographers, and visitors combining Jaisalmer with desert experiences.

**Voice:** Warm, specific, calm, hospitable, culturally grounded. Avoid exaggerated luxury language, false scarcity, “best in Rajasthan” claims, and pressure-heavy sales copy.

## Homepage sequence

1. **Hero — instant recognition:** “Stay inside the Golden Fort.” Subheading explains the differentiator: a family-run haveli inside Jaisalmer Fort, rooftop sunsets and local hospitality. Primary CTA: “Plan your stay”. Secondary CTA: “Explore the experiences”.
2. **Booking enquiry strip:** Arrival, departure and guest count. Date validation then a WhatsApp message. It is an enquiry, not an instant booking or payment.
3. **Place/story:** Explain what living inside the fort feels like and set guest expectations.
4. **Experiences:** Desert safari/camping, city tour, rooftop dining.
5. **Rooms:** Heritage character, current availability by direct enquiry, and factual amenities only.
6. **Dining:** Rooftop restaurant and candlelit dinner enquiries.
7. **Why us:** Inside the fort, local guidance, several trip experiences in one conversation.
8. **Proof:** Lead with verifiable facts (hospitality journey began in 2005, inside the fort, rooftop dining, direct local guidance) and link to current public guest reviews. No fabricated rating or testimonial is shown. Add excerpts only after permission and attribution checks.
9. **FAQ:** Fort access, luggage help, tours, reservations.
10. **Final CTA:** “The Golden City is calling. Come feel it.” WhatsApp enquiry.

## Hero copy

**Headline:** Stay inside the Golden Fort.

**Subheadline:** Come home to a family-run haveli within the living walls of Jaisalmer Fort—where old sandstone, rooftop sunsets and the warmth of local hospitality make the journey as memorable as the destination.

**Primary CTA:** Plan your stay.

**Secondary CTA:** Explore the experiences.

## Service page messages

- **Rooms:** “A room with a story to tell.” Lead with distinctive traditional character and comfort; ask the team to confirm the exact room, inclusions, bed arrangement and current rate.
- **Desert safari / camping:** “See the desert. Hear the stories.” Describe sunset camel safari and overnight/Swiss-tent options as enquiries whose inclusions and rates must be confirmed.
- **Guided city tours:** “Explore at your own pace.” Ask travellers what interests them and offer half-day/full-day enquiry options and language availability.
- **Rooftop dining:** “Good food. Golden hour.” Encourage reservations for special occasions, while confirming the current menu, hours, price and candlelit arrangements.
- **About:** “Our roots are here. Our doors are open.” Connect the family’s local roots and 2005 hospitality journey with what guests want: a more personal experience of Jaisalmer.
- **Arrival:** “Inside the fort. Here to help.” Explain the final walk before booking rather than surprising guests at arrival.

## CTA principles

- Use one clear primary action per section.
- Use “Check availability” for room enquiries, not “Book now” when a live booking engine is not connected.
- Use WhatsApp for low-friction direct communication; retain telephone and map links.
- Ask for only useful enquiry information before the first response.
- Do not add countdown timers, fake limited-room notices, fabricated review scores, or invented testimonials.
