# Guest testimonial collection

The website intentionally does not publish invented quotes. To add a guest-proof section:

1. Select genuine reviews from Google, Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or messages the hotel is authorised to quote.
2. Preserve the meaning and keep any edited extract faithful to the original.
3. Attribute the review to the original platform and use the guest's publicly displayed first name/initial only when allowed by that platform and the guest's expectations.
4. Ask permission before republishing private messages or personal photographs.
5. Link to the review source where possible; don't imply that old or third-party scores are live Google ratings.
6. Avoid incentives for positive reviews and never remove critical feedback solely because it is negative.

## Suggested prompts to ask recent guests (not publish as reviews)

- What made you choose a stay inside Jaisalmer Fort?
- What do you remember most about the rooftop or the team's welcome?
- What should a future traveller know before booking?

Use responses only after the guest approves the final wording and attribution.
