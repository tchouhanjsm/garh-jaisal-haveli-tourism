Your last few minutes

The fort gate is close.
We’ll help from there.

From the fort entrance, the haveli is a short walk through the lanes. From the fort parking area, allow a few minutes on foot. Busy periods can restrict rickshaw access, so please contact us if you need assistance or have heavy luggage.

Best next step: WhatsApp us when your train, car or flight is due to arrive. We’ll confirm the most useful meeting point and current arrangements.

Arrival FAQs

The useful details,
all in one place.

Can a car drop me directly at the hotel?+

No. Cars cannot reach the hotel door inside the fort. Please use the fort parking area as the drop-off point and contact us for final walking directions.

Can the team help carry our luggage?+

Assistance can be coordinated. Please share your ETA ahead of time or call us when you reach the fort parking area so we can confirm arrangements.

What if I arrive after reception hours?+

Please call or WhatsApp ahead to discuss late-night or very early arrival arrangements. We recommend at least a day’s notice where possible.

Is the route suitable for guests with mobility needs?+

The route includes narrow, uneven stone lanes and some steps. Please contact us before booking so we can discuss the route and the help available for your specific needs.

Can you help arrange a transfer from the station or airport?+

Ask us about current transfer options and prices. Please confirm the service, pickup point, fare and timing directly with the team before travelling.
