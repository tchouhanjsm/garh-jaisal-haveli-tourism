A home with a history

Inside the fort.
Close to the city.

Garh Jaisal Haveli is a heritage stay inside Jaisalmer Fort. The sandstone architecture, narrow lanes and everyday life within the fort shape the setting visitors encounter here.

A living fort, not a hotel district

Stay within
the old walls.

Garh Jaisal Haveli is inside Jaisalmer Fort, among sandstone lanes, homes, shops and the everyday life of the historic settlement.

The setting is different from a hotel reached by driveway. Cars cannot reach the door, and the final approach is on foot. Read the arrival guide before travelling and confirm the meeting point for your route.

Compare the seven named rooms, then ask for current rates, room assignment and amenities. The photographs show room character; they are not live inventory.

What to expect from the setting

A heritage stay
with practical details.

The lanes and sandstone architecture are part of everyday life inside the fort. Plan for a walk from the agreed meeting point rather than expecting a vehicle to arrive at the door.

We want the useful details to be clear: which room is offered, what it costs, how you will arrive and what a tour or meal includes. Rates, menu items, service hours and date-specific arrangements should be confirmed before you rely on them.

That is a more honest introduction to the haveli than making broad promises about services that may vary by date.

Spend time in the fort lanes, look out over the sandstone rooftops and choose how much you want to explore beyond the walls.

For the practical side, confirm the room, current rates, final walking route, any tour that is operating and today's dining options before setting out.
