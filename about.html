A home with a history

Our roots are here.
Our doors are open.

Garh Jaisal Haveli grew from a connection to Jaisalmer and a wish to share the feeling of living inside its remarkable fort.

A family story, in golden stone

A place to stay.
A place to belong.

Garh Jaisal Haveli is more than an address inside the fort. It is part of a place our family has long called home.

Our connection to Jaisalmer comes with a deep affection for its sandstone architecture, its living lanes and the everyday traditions that make the Golden City distinctive. We welcome travellers who want to understand the fort as a lived-in place, not only a landmark.

Today, we welcome guests who arrive curious about Rajasthan and hope they leave with more than photographs: a little local context, a rooftop memory, a favourite meal or a story they carry onward.

The people behind the welcome

Hospitality is
personal.

We believe the little things matter: helping someone find their way through the fort, explaining the etiquette of a local place, sharing which hour makes the sandstone glow, or listening to what a guest hopes to discover.

Our on-site team brings the day-to-day welcome to life, while our family remains closely connected to the property. We keep improving the stay through practical feedback, thoughtful upkeep and a respect for the character of the haveli.

That is the spirit we hope you feel here—not a performance of royalty, but genuine hospitality shaped by local roots.

What we want you to take away

A little closer
to Jaisalmer.

We want your time here to feel easy to navigate and rich in discoveries. Ask questions, tell us what you love, and let us help you connect your stay with city tours, desert adventures and rooftop dining.

We’ll always aim to be clear about room details, rates, arrival arrangements and what each experience includes—so you can plan confidently and enjoy the time you have.
