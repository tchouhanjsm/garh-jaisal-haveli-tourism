A living fort

Not a resort set apart from the city.

The haveli sits within Jaisalmer Fort, where everyday life continues alongside historic sandstone architecture.

The lanes connect homes, shops and the routes people use each day. Walk thoughtfully, leave room for residents and other visitors, and treat the fort as a lived-in neighbourhood—not a backdrop built only for photographs.

That context is what makes the address distinctive. It also means arrival needs more planning than a hotel reached by driveway: cars cannot reach the door, so the final approach is on foot through narrow lanes and uneven stone.

The pace of the place

Look beyond
the doorway.

The fort is more than a backdrop: its lanes connect homes, shops and the routines of a living settlement.

Take time to notice the sandstone details and rooftops, while leaving room for people moving through the lanes. Follow local signs and ask before photographing residents or private spaces.

The rooftop and interior photographs on this site show the spaces pictured; they do not confirm which dining or other services are operating on a particular date. Ask before making plans around a meal, tour or special arrangement.
