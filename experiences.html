Useful details before you enquire

Know what to confirm for your date.

Desert and city experiences are not interchangeable. Before choosing, ask for the route, sequence, time needed, transport, fees, inclusions, exclusions and cancellation terms that apply to your requested date. This page does not claim live availability.

Swiss-tent stay details

Owner-confirmed Swiss-tent facilities include a king-size bed, attached bathroom with hot and cold shower, tea and coffee maker, toiletries, study table, full-length mirror and towels. Confirm the exact tent, current availability and package inclusions for your date; these details do not apply to every desert option.

A focused Fort walk

The planned Jaisalmer Fort walk focuses on Jain temples inside the fort, the Palace Museum and a heritage street walk. Confirm the route, access arrangements, current price and guide-language availability for your date.

Guide language

The owner’s recorded guide-language list is English, Hindi, Bengali, Italian, French, Spanish and German. A specific language must be confirmed for the requested date; please ask before relying on availability.

Longer road journeys

The owner records SUV and sedan arrangements across Rajasthan and Delhi, with drivers able to communicate in Hindi and English. Ask whether your route and preferred vehicle can be arranged; route, vehicle and availability are confirmed per request, not guaranteed by this page.
