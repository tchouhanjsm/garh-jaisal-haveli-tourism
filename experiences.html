Some travellers come for the fort’s history. Others come for the desert horizon. We can help you discuss both, then shape the plan around the time you have.

01 / The Thar Desert

Desert safari & overnight stays

Leave the bustle of the old city behind for open landscapes, sunset light and the quiet of the desert after dark. Ask about sunset camel safari options, overnight camping and Swiss-tent stays, including what is included in each package.

Tell us your travel dates, preferred pace and whether you’d like a short outing or an overnight experience; we’ll share the current options before you decide.

Ask about desert options ↗
02 / Stories in sandstone

Guided Jaisalmer city tours

See more than the postcard view. Explore the fort, heritage architecture, local landmarks and the character of the old city with a guide who can give the places context.

Choose a half-day or full-day tour and ask about available guide languages. Share your interests—history, architecture, local life or photography—and we’ll help discuss an itinerary.

Plan a city tour ↗
03 / Golden hour

Rooftop moments

Pause above the fort for a sunset meal, a relaxed evening or a special dinner. The changing light over the sandstone city is part of the experience.

For an anniversary, celebration or candlelit table, please enquire in advance so the team can discuss the arrangements and menu options.

04 / Make it yours

A trip shaped around you

Travelling as a couple, with family or with a few free days between destinations? Let us know what matters most to you and how much time you have in Jaisalmer.

We’ll help you connect the dots between your stay, the old city, rooftop dining and available desert experiences—without overfilling your schedule.
