Source concept 01

Sunset camel safari

The legacy itinerary describes jeep travel via Khuldhara, a natural pond and a nomadic village, followed by camel riding, sunset tea/coffee and snacks, and a desert dinner before returning to Jaisalmer. Confirm the current route and inclusions for your date.

Duration & timing

Transport & access

Request written details for route and stops, refreshments or meals, entry fees, extras and cancellation terms.

Duration & timing
Confirm whether an overnight stay is currently available and ask for the arrival, programme and departure windows.
Transport & access

Inclusions & exclusions
Confirm meals, camel riding, any cultural programme, transfers, fees, drinks, extras and cancellation terms in writing.

Half-day guided Jaisalmer tour

The source catalogue describes a half-day guided introduction to the Fort, havelis, heritage lanes and markets. Owner Facts also records a planned Fort walk focused on Jain temples inside the fort, the Palace Museum and a heritage street walk. Confirm the route, access, guide language and format for your date.

Duration & timing
Ask whether a half-day format is currently offered, the total walking time and the meeting and finish points.
Transport & access
Confirm walking distance, steps, mobility considerations, any vehicle use, entry rules and site access for your date.
Inclusions & exclusions
Ask whether a guide and requested language are available, and which entry fees, transport, meals or tickets cost extra.

Source concept 06

Full-day guided Jaisalmer tour

The source catalogue describes a fuller guided day through the Fort, havelis, markets and heritage areas, with the actual stops agreed for the trip. English, Hindi, Bengali, Italian, French, Spanish and German are listed as guide languages in source facts, but availability must be confirmed. The image is a heritage reference, not a promised stop.

Duration & timing
Confirm whether a full-day format is offered, the start and finish window, site opening hours and rest breaks.
Transport & access
Ask which stops are walkable, whether a vehicle is needed, and what meeting point, entry rules and mobility limits apply.
Inclusions & exclusions
Request an itemized list for guide, transport, entry fees, meals, tickets, personal purchases and cancellation terms.
