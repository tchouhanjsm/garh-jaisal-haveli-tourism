Half-day guided Jaisalmer tour

The source catalogue describes a half-day guided introduction to the Fort, havelis, heritage lanes and markets. Confirm the route, access, guide language and format for your date.

Ask whether a guide and requested language are available, and which entry fees, transport, meals or tickets cost extra.

Full-day guided Jaisalmer tour

The source catalogue describes a fuller guided day through the Fort, havelis, markets and heritage areas, with the actual stops agreed for the trip.
