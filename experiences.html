Know what to confirm for your date.

Desert and city experiences are not interchangeable. Before choosing, ask for the route, sequence, time needed, transport, fees, inclusions, exclusions and cancellation terms that apply to your requested date. This page does not claim live availability.

Swiss-tent stay details

Owner-confirmed Swiss-tent facilities include a king-size bed, attached bathroom with hot and cold shower, tea and coffee maker, toiletries, study table, full-length mirror and towels. Confirm the exact tent, current availability and package inclusions for your date; these details do not apply to every desert option.
